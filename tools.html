Tools

Three small machines. None of them will do your homework, and none of them is trying to teach you anything — they are here because watching a thing run is a different kind of understanding from reading about it.

Prime factoriser

Every whole number above 1 breaks into primes in exactly one way. Give it a number and watch it come apart.

Prime factors appear here.

Fractal tree

A trunk that splits into two smaller copies of itself, which split again, and again. Nothing here knows what a tree looks like — the shape is entirely a consequence of the two numbers below.

26°
0.72
2

Equation grapher

Type a function of x and it gets drawn. It will also go looking for the places the curve crosses the axis — the roots of f(x) = 0 — and mark them, because those are the points worth arguing about.

Roots appear here.

Understands + − * / ^, brackets, and sin, cos, tan, sqrt, abs, ln, log, exp, along with π as pi and e. Implied multiplication is fine: 3x and 2sin(x) both work.
