NumberOnWings

Hello, fancy meeting you here! You probably have questions. Most of them are below.

What the heck is NumberOnWings?!▼

A personal site for mathematics. Puzzles, experiments, programs, and whatever is being chased at the moment. It is not a course. There are no lessons, no levels, no progress bar, and nothing here will ask you to sign up for anything.

Who is the π with eyes?▼

That is IrAcoNAl — say it ir-ra-tion-AL. Painted by hand, permanently unimpressed, and mathematically incapable of being written as a fraction. The mascot, and the reason half the design is built around a circle.

Is this going to teach me something?▼

Maybe, but that is a side effect rather than the point. Nothing here is simplified for the sake of being easy to swallow. If an answer needs the axiom of choice or a field extension to be honest, it gets one.

Why does the site look like it just started?▼

Because it did. The previous NumberOnWings was taken down to the ground and this one is being written from nothing — new structure, new everything. The old one is archived, not deleted.

Why is everything orange?▼

Because orange was picked on purpose. Yellow lifts it, and red appears exactly once on this page. It is already on screen if you are reading this. Go find it.

So what is actually here right now?▼

This page. That is the honest answer. Things get added when they are finished rather than when they are announced, and the menu at the top fills in as pages appear.
