Don't click here

There is a button below. It does not want to be pressed, and it will do something about that. Chase it anyway — and keep half an eye on the numbers in the corner while you do.

dodges 0 · distance travelled 0 px

You caught it.

Of course you did. Look at what it was doing: every dodge carried it exactly half as far as the one before.

Suppose it had never given up and dodged forever — infinitely many times. Add every one of those infinitely many jumps together and the total is not infinite. It is , and not one pixel more:

a + ar + ar2 + ar3 + … = a / (1 − r),   for |r| < 1

With that comes to twice the first jump. The button had a finite amount of running in it from the very first move, so the chase could only ever end one way. It was never escaping. It was converging.

This is Zeno's paradox with the mystery removed. Infinitely many steps do not need infinitely much room — they only need to shrink fast enough, and halving is fast enough.
